## Decisions

- **One frame every 2 min** (P027: adjacent 30 s frames are 82-92 % the same scene); the full-rate frames stay on disk for P075 to densify if a curve asks for it.
- **Nearest 30 s grid point within 15 s** of the P036 table carries the label; the +-30 s window behind each grid point is the P031 tolerance. Frames on the grid (gap 0 s) are the rule, not the exception.
- **The ceilometer looks at the zenith; the model must too.** Every pair records the zenith pixel from the Sun-fitted pose and the radius of a 10-degree cone around it; pairs whose zenith falls outside the camera mask are excluded from the splits. Calibrations without a fitted pose (status ok) are refused.
- **Night frames are not in the splits** (80 ms exposure regime; daytime model), but stay in `data/cbh_pairs.parquet` with daytime = False.
- **Days are the units, shared across OLDLR and WESTE** (P027/P037); days the in-domain Eye2Sky split already assigned keep that split; the others are assigned 70/10/20 with the day's dominant label as the stratum.
- **Overcast-day guard:** `day_label_share` (share of the day's pairs with the same label) travels with every pair; P075 down-weights by it or caps per day. The per-month table shows where the low-cloud mass sits.
- **Synthetic views (P049) adopt this rule:** `inherit_label` is replaced by a join to this table's pairs by source frame.
